Calculation details
Gross area
Geodesic polygon area from the mapped pasture boundary.
Excluded area
Area where each assigned exclusion overlaps its parent pasture. Portions outside the pasture are not subtracted.
Net measured area
Gross area − overlapping exclusions.
Estimated usable area
Net measured × (1 − brush/timber %) × (1 − slope reduction %) × (1 − inaccessible %). User-entered reductions are applied sequentially.
Perimeter
Geodesic length around each mapped pasture boundary. Project perimeter is the sum of pasture perimeters.
